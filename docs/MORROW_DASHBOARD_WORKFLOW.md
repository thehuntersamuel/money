# Using PACT Finance and Morrow

The objective is disciplined, source-backed decisions with measurable forward results. Neither a professional-looking dashboard, a paid feed nor an AI model guarantees profitable trades. Morrow remains an advisory research and paper-evaluation system. Real orders, transfers, strategy activation and subscription changes are outside this release.

## 1. Open the correct book and check evidence

Open Trade and select **Robinhood Savings**, the configured Morrow paper book. Distinguish it from real investment balances or another paper book. Start with the provider cards, latest saved research and Operations. A live price, a saved research decision and a job receipt answer different questions. Read their timestamps independently.

If a feed is stale, stop only decisions that require that missing input. Company filings and appropriately dated history can still support research. A green feed card does not approve a trade; a historical job failure does not by itself prove that the feed is currently broken. Never trade from the reference watchlist's price alone.

## 2. Let discovery search beyond the watchlist

The intended morning workflow uses the existing 09:45 ET weekday job. It can search supported US equities/ETFs, use broad Tiingo news to discover candidates, verify ticker metadata, and then request ticker-specific news and dated history. Directory listings are not trade recommendations. A reference watchlist is a research list, not a portfolio or an exhaustive ranking of the market.

Keep the existing maximum of five full candidate reviews, with the two strongest causal theses investigated deeply. More API calls and more trades do not imply better decisions. For each potentially useful included capability, ask which decision it improves. Options, crypto, analyst consensus and other products are not assumed to be included or verified.

## 3. Read a complete company review

Expand **Research notes**. Require a plain-language explanation of what the company does, why profits or cash generation might change, what the market may be missing, the dated catalyst, valuation assumptions, the strongest bear case, and the condition that would invalidate the idea. Open the linked original filing or issuer release for material claims. Two URLs that repeat one press release are not two independent facts.

Compare source publication, event, retrieval and research times. Old news rediscovered today is not a new catalyst. Missing consensus or valuation data stays missing. A fallen stock price alone is not a thesis. Watching and passing are valid research outcomes; neither should be forced into a trade.

## 4. Follow the decision and next review

The activity list and notes show watch, pass, qualified-proposal or paper-position states. Review the reasons and due date. The source-linked proposal view is not an execution receipt. New reviews should supersede earlier thinking by stable idea key while preserving history. A changed thesis needs a new review/version, not a silent edit to an old forecast.

Saved tickers enter the reference watchlist. The worker's existing bounded configuration provides detailed SIP monitoring for active candidates and open paper positions, plus research bars for broader supported coverage. A minute bar cannot prove intraminute event order, a trigger crossing or an executable fill.

## 5. Define the paper experiment before opening it

The current new-entry gate stays closed. Before a separately approved paper evaluation, the implementation owner must demonstrate fresh relevant prices and coverage, working durable trigger/review handling, close receipts, consistent limits, and actual job execution/synchronization. Freeze a dated strategy version, eligible universe, horizon, entry/exit rules, benchmark, costs and evaluation criteria. The AI cannot activate its own strategy.

Existing Morrow policy specifies planned risk of 0.5% per position and 1% aggregate, at most three open positions and one new opening per New York trading day, with both stated and implied reward-to-risk at least 1.5:1. These are existing limits, not a recommendation to use the maximum. Price gaps can exceed planned stop losses. A qualified proposal or favorable ratio alone is insufficient.

## 6. Monitor and reconcile

The intended intraday monitor checks at :15 and :45 during its existing weekday window and requests a review only when relevant state changes. A trigger is a wake-up for review, not an order. Existing exit obligations remain in force. Use the 16:20 ET closing job to reconcile proposals, simulated positions, closes, receipts, missed intervals and overdue reviews; respect the actual exchange calendar and half-days.

Read Operations for the actual job finish, save time, provider, model and reasoning. Configuration alone does not prove hosted execution. Missing receipts, fallback runs or unknown routing must be labeled and excluded from claims about the intended model's performance.

## 7. Measure quality after costs

Use the Experiment lab and ROI panels to compare mature forward paper outcomes against a predeclared passive/cash benchmark and simple mechanical alternative with matching exposure and dates. Include spreads, slippage, fees and allocated data/hosting/model costs. Keep research-only, rejected and simulated-trade cohorts separate. Unknown inputs must not become zero costs or profit.

Look at drawdown, concentration, the result with the best trade removed, regime dependence and missing outcomes. One profitable month or a small number of correlated wins does not establish an edge. Paid-data value is whether decisions improve after its costs, not whether requests succeed.

## 8. Improve without changing the historical test

The intended Saturday 11:00 ET lab proposes at most one falsifiable change. Keep a challenger separate from the frozen strategy and record unsuccessful variants. Sunday 18:00 ET is the executive review of process, failures, costs, and owner decisions. These are the documented intended schedules; verify them against the Mac's actual jobs and receipts.

The October 26 retrospective is a review backstop, not automatic maturity or real-money approval. Acceptable conclusions include continue paper testing, redesign or stop. Any later real-money decision remains Hunter's separate decision based on risk capacity and forward evidence; the dashboard never automatically mirrors a trade.
